The Copperplate

Your name worked out as numbers, three cards drawn against a question, and one reading that puts them together.

First

The name you were given

Numerology reads character from the full name recorded at birth — first, any middle, and last. That's the one to use here.

Why the birth name: in the Pythagorean system the name you were first given is treated as the blueprint — the character you arrived with. A married name or nickname describes how you're read now, not what you're made of. If you go by a different name entirely, run it a second time and compare.

Your numbers

The arithmetic of a name

Each letter carries a value, one through nine. Vowels are struck in copper — they feed the private number. Consonants are struck in bone — they feed the public one.

Vowels → Soul Urge Consonants → Personality All letters → Expression

Second

Hold a question

Ask something open. "What am I not seeing about this job?" reads better than "will I get the job?" — the cards describe a situation, they don't vote on it.
